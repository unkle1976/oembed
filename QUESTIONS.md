## Health (answer before the plan is final)

1. **Do you have any diagnosed conditions or take any medication?** Statins, blood pressure tablets, metformin, anything for thyroid, antidepressants. Several of these change the advice.
2. **Do you know your blood pressure or any recent cholesterol numbers?** Even old ones help. If not, booking an NHS Health Check is the first action.
3. **Any symptoms when you exert yourself?** Chest tightness, unusual breathlessness, dizziness. Yes to any means see the GP before running, not after.
4. **Family history?** Heart attack or stroke in a parent or sibling before 60 raises your risk and changes the statin conversation.
5. **Do you smoke or vape?**
6. **Alcohol: how much in a typical week, honestly?** This changes both the calorie plan and the triglyceride picture. No judgement, it just needs to be in the plan rather than outside it.
7. **Any joint problems?** Knees, Achilles, hips, back. Changes the running progression.

## Food (shapes the meal plan)

8. **Who cooks, and how much time do you have on a weekday evening?** Fifteen minutes or an hour makes a different plan.
9. **Are you cooking for one, or for a family who eat differently?**
10. **Anything you will not eat?** Fish, eggs, dairy, beans, specific vegetables. Allergies or intolerances.
11. **What did the "rubbish" year actually look like?** Takeaways, snacks, drinks, meal deals, late-night eating. The plan should attack the biggest source first.
12. **When are you hungriest, and when do you tend to overeat?** Evenings, weekends, after work, at the desk.
13. **Do you eat breakfast?**
14. **Budget constraints?** Rough weekly food spend you are happy with.
15. **Lunches: at home, at a desk, out?**

## Training

16. **When did you last run regularly, and what was your marathon time?** Gives a sense of what your body remembers.
17. **Days and times you can realistically train.** Three runs and two gym sessions is the plan. If that is four sessions total, say so.
18. **Gym access or home weights only?** Hevy works for both, but the strength plan differs.
19. **Do you have a watch or HR monitor?** Garmin, Apple Watch, Fitbit. Changes how we set easy-run intensity.

## Tracking

20. **MyFitnessPal, or do you want Claude to be the tracker?** Recommendation in the nutrition plan. Short version: use an app for the daily logging, use this repo and Claude for weekly review and adjustment. An app has the barcode scanner and the UK food database. Claude is better at the "why isn't this working" conversation.
