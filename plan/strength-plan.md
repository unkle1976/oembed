# Strength plan

Built from `research/running.md` section 5. Two sessions a week, 40 to 50 minutes, logged in Hevy. This is not optional. In trials of older adults with obesity, adding resistance training to a calorie deficit prevented about 93 percent of the muscle loss seen with diet alone. From 50 onwards you lose roughly 1 percent of muscle a year and strength faster than that; a deficit speeds that up unless you lift.

## Scheduling

On non-running days, or after an easy run. Not the day before your longest run. A sample week once running is established:

| Mon | Tue | Wed | Thu | Fri | Sat | Sun |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Run | Strength A | Rest or walk | Run | Strength B | Rest or walk | Run |

## Session A

| Exercise | Sets x reps | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Goblet squat or leg press | 3 x 8 to 12 | Depth you can control |
| Romanian deadlift (dumbbell or bar) | 3 x 8 to 12 | Hinge, soft knees, hamstrings loaded |
| Step-up onto a box or bench | 2 x 10 each leg | Single-leg control for knees and hips |
| Standing calf raise, straight knee | 3 x 10 to 15, slow | Heavy and slow. This is Achilles insurance |
| Dumbbell row or cable row | 3 x 8 to 12 | |
| Plank | 3 x 30 to 45 s | Anti-extension core |

## Session B

| Exercise | Sets x reps | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Split squat or reverse lunge | 3 x 8 to 10 each leg | |
| Hip thrust or glute bridge | 3 x 10 to 12 | |
| Seated calf raise, bent knee | 3 x 10 to 15, slow | Loads soleus, the other half of the Achilles |
| Dumbbell bench press or push-up | 3 x 8 to 12 | |
| Lat pulldown or assisted pull-up | 3 x 8 to 12 | |
| Dead bug or Pallof press | 3 x 10 each side | Anti-rotation core |

## Progression

Pick a weight where the last two reps are hard but clean. When you hit the top of the rep range on all sets, add the smallest increment. Hevy tracks this. Expect fast gains for the first 8 to 12 weeks; most of that is your nervous system, and it is real.

## Protein and creatine

Lifting is what makes the 120 to 150 g protein target worth hitting. Creatine monohydrate at 3 to 5 g a day improves strength and lean-mass gains at 50+ and is safe with normal kidneys. See `plan/nutrition-plan.md`.

## If you only have home equipment

Swap: goblet squat with a kettlebell or rucksack; RDL with dumbbells; calf raises on a step; push-ups; rows with a resistance band or a rucksack; hip thrusts with feet on a chair. Single-leg calf raises off a step, slow, 3 x 10 to 15, are the one thing you must not drop.

## Adaptation

Tendons and bone adapt in months, not weeks. If a lift hurts a joint rather than a muscle, swap it. Soreness the next day is fine; pain that alters how you walk is not.
