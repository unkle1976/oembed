# Week 1 menu: Sunday 8 to Saturday 14 November 2026

Built for efficiency: one batch cook on Sunday, two breakfasts on repeat, every ingredient used at least twice, nothing that goes off before Saturday. Calories and protein are estimates from standard UK food composition values. Your app will give exact figures for the products you buy.

Daily target about 1600 kcal and 130 g protein. MyFitnessPal targets: 1600 kcal, 130 g protein, 53 g fat, 150 g carbs, 30 g fibre, saturated fat under 15 g, sodium under 2,400 mg (6 g salt). Run days: 1800 kcal, carbs up to 200 g.

**Updated 8 Nov for blood pressure.** Home readings on 7 and 8 Nov had the bottom number at 88 to 93, above the home threshold of 85. Salt is now the main food target, so this version cuts hidden salt, adds 20 g of unsalted nuts a day (the nutrition plan's LDL stack) and adds raw veg to lunches for potassium. Calories and protein stay on target. On a run day, add about 150 to 200 kcal: an apple and an extra slice of toast is enough in week 1.

## Sunday batch cook (about 1 hour, after the shop)

Shopping list with current prices: `plan/week-1-shopping.md`.

1. **Lentil soup, 4 portions** (lunches Mon to Thu). Soften 1 onion, 2 carrots, 2 cloves garlic in 1 tsp oil. Add 250 g red lentils, 1 tin chopped tomatoes, 1.2 L stock from 1 reduced-salt cube (or half a normal cube), 1 tsp cumin, an extra clove of garlic for flavour. Don't add salt. Simmer 25 minutes, blend half if you like it thick. Fridge 2 portions, freeze 2. Freeze half the bag of fresh spinach too, for Friday's fish.
2. **Chilli, 4 portions** (dinners Mon and Wed, 2 to the freezer for next week). Brown 500 g 5% mince, add 1 onion, a handful of frozen peppers, 1 carrot, garlic, 1 tsp each cumin, paprika and chilli. Add 2 tins chopped tomatoes and 1 tin kidney beans, drained and rinsed. No stock cube or salt; the spices carry it. Simmer 30 minutes.
3. **Boil 4 eggs** for the soup lunches.
4. **Cook 100 g brown rice** for the two chilli dinners and keep it in the fridge. Cool it quickly and eat within a day or two, or freeze it.

## Salt rules for this week

- No salt in cooking or at the table. Use garlic, lemon, chilli, cumin, paprika and black pepper
- Reduced-salt stock cube and reduced-salt soy sauce only, and measure the soy
- Drain and rinse all tinned beans and chickpeas
- Tinned fish in tomato sauce, olive oil or spring water, never brine. The tins on the list already are
- Bread is one of the biggest salt sources in the UK diet, so keep to the slices in the plan
