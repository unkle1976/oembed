# Week 1 menu: Sunday 8 to Saturday 14 November 2026

Built for efficiency: one batch cook on Sunday, two breakfasts on repeat, every ingredient used at least twice, nothing that goes off before Saturday. Calories and protein are estimates from standard UK food composition values. Your app will give exact figures for the products you buy.

Daily target about 1600 kcal and 130 g protein. On a run day, add about 150 to 200 kcal: an apple and an extra slice of toast is enough in week 1.

## Sunday batch cook (about 1 hour, after the shop)

Shopping list with current prices: `plan/week-1-shopping.md`.

1. **Lentil soup, 4 portions** (lunches Mon to Thu). Soften 1 onion, 2 carrots, 2 cloves garlic in 1 tsp oil. Add 250 g red lentils, 1 tin chopped tomatoes, 1.2 L stock from 1 cube, 1 tsp cumin. Simmer 25 minutes, blend half if you like it thick. Fridge 2 portions, freeze 2. Freeze half the bag of fresh spinach too, for Friday's fish.
2. **Chilli, 4 portions** (dinners Mon and Wed, 2 to the freezer for next week). Brown 500 g 5% mince, add 1 onion, a handful of frozen peppers, 1 carrot, garlic, 1 tsp each cumin, paprika and chilli. Add 2 tins chopped tomatoes and 1 tin kidney beans. Simmer 30 minutes.
3. **Boil 4 eggs** for the soup lunches.
4. **Cook 100 g brown rice** for the two chilli dinners and keep it in the fridge. Cool it quickly and eat within a day or two, or freeze it.

## The two breakfasts

- **Porridge** (about 400 kcal, 26 g protein): 50 g oats cooked in 250 ml semi-skimmed milk, 100 g frozen berries stirred in, 100 g 0% Greek yogurt on top. Make it as overnight oats in a jar if mornings are rushed.
- **Omelette** (about 390 kcal, 26 g protein): 3 eggs, a handful of frozen spinach, a tomato, 1 tsp oil, 1 slice wholemeal toast.
