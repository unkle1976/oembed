## Where the savings are

- **Mince.** Two 250 g packs cost £5.50. The 500 g pack is £7.00 today.
- **Chicken.** The diced 640 g pack is the right size for three dinners, and Nectar takes it to £4.50. The 1 kg fresh pack is out of stock everywhere, and the 1 kg frozen pack is £7.00.
- **Oil.** Vegetable oil is £1.45 a litre, against £7.25 for a litre of olive oil. Sainsbury's vegetable oil is normally 100% rapeseed, which is as good as olive oil for cholesterol, but check the label says rapeseed. Keep olive oil for when it's on offer.
- **Lemons.** Trolley only lists organic lemons at £1.90 for 3. A bottle of lemon juice is £0.90 and lasts weeks.
- **Sardines.** Sainsbury's own-brand sardines are out of stock. Princes are £1.30. If the own brand is back on the shelf, take it.
- **Protein powder.** Fat-free Greek yogurt is the cheapest protein on this list. Most UK fat-free Greek-style yogurts carry 8 to 10 g of protein per 100 g, so check the label on the tub. At £1.70 a kilo, it works out at roughly 45 to 55p per 25 g of protein, which is cheaper than the whey prices the research agent found online. That's why the menu uses a second yogurt snack instead of cottage cheese or a shake.
- **Salmon.** At £6.00 it's the dearest line. A second tin of mackerel instead saves £4.80 this week and still gives you two oily-fish meals.
- **Creatine.** Sci-Mx at £10 for 250 g is the only creatine Sainsbury's lists. The research agent saw Myprotein Creapure creatine at about £17 for 500 g online, which is cheaper per dose, but I couldn't check that price from here.

## Not on this list, and why

- **Salad leaves.** They'd wilt before Friday. Cucumber and cherry tomatoes cover the salad.
- **Frozen peas, broccoli and spinach.** Trolley doesn't list Sainsbury's own versions, so I couldn't price them. The 1 kg bag of mixed vegetables covers those meals. Freeze half the fresh spinach on Sunday for Friday's fish.
- **Peanut butter.** Optional. Sainsbury's 100% Smooth Peanut Butter is £2.35 for 340 g. The nuts are now on the list.
- **Psyllium.** Sainsbury's only stocks branded fibre sachets at £16 for 30. Plain psyllium husk powder is far cheaper online. The research agent saw about £10 to £13 for 500 g but couldn't verify it.
- **Plant sterol drinks.** These are optional, per the nutrition plan. The research agent saw Sainsbury's own cholesterol-lowering drinks at about £3 for 6 but couldn't verify the price.
