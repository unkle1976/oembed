# Nutrition plan

Built from `research/cholesterol.md`, `research/weight-loss.md` and `research/nutrients.md`. Numbers in `profile.md`. This is version 1, written before your answers to `QUESTIONS.md`; expect it to change.

## The five rules

1. **1600 kcal on rest days, 1800 to 1900 on run days.** The deficit does the weight loss. Running days get extra because a 5 km run at 96 kg costs about 480 kcal and 1600 minus that is too low for a man your size.
2. **30 to 40 g protein at each of 3 to 4 meals, 120 to 150 g a day.** Protects muscle while you lose, and is the most reliable hunger control.
3. **A plant staple at every meal, 30 g fibre a day.** Oats, lentils, beans, wholemeal bread, vegetables, fruit. Most UK men manage about 20 g. Fibre is also the main food lever on LDL after saturated fat.
4. **Swap saturated fat for unsaturated, do not just cut it.** Butter to olive or rapeseed oil. Fatty meat to fish, chicken, beans. Cheese as a flavouring, not a block. Target under 15 g saturated fat a day. This is the single biggest dietary lever on LDL.
5. **Nothing ultra-processed as a default, and no takeaways for the first four weeks.** Not because UPF is poison, but because the trial evidence shows people eat about 500 kcal a day more on it without noticing, and about 75 percent of UK salt comes from processed food and takeaways.

## Cholesterol-specific additions (Heart UK UCLP stack)

Add these on top of the five rules. Each one moves LDL by roughly 3 to 10 percent, and the effects are broadly additive. Together with the saturated-fat swap, 10 to 20 percent is realistic free-living.

| Food | Daily amount | Expected LDL effect | How |
|---|---|---|---|
| Oats or barley (beta-glucan) | 3 g beta-glucan, which is about 50 to 60 g oats | about 5 to 7 percent | Porridge, overnight oats, oats in yogurt |
| Nuts | 30 g (a small handful) | about 4 percent | Almonds, walnuts, peanuts. Weigh them; 30 g is 180 kcal |
| Soya | 25 g soya protein | about 3 to 4 percent | Soya milk on porridge, tofu, edamame. Optional if you dislike it |
| Plant sterol or stanol foods | 1.5 to 2.4 g | about 8 percent | Benecol or Flora ProActiv spread or mini-drinks. NICE does not recommend them for lack of outcome data; Heart UK and ESC accept them. Your call. They lower LDL; nobody has shown they prevent heart attacks |
| Psyllium husk | 10 g | about 0.33 mmol/L | Stirred into water or yogurt. Also gets you to 30 g fibre on the days food falls short |
| Oily fish | 2 portions a week (140 g each) | triglycerides, not LDL | Tinned sardines, mackerel, salmon. Eat the fish, skip the capsules |

## Supplements

| Take | Dose | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Vitamin D | 10 to 25 ug (400 to 1000 IU) daily, October to March at least | NHS advice for everyone in UK winter. You are starting in November after a poor year. Do not exceed 100 ug |
| Creatine monohydrate | 3 to 5 g daily, no loading | Better strength and lean-mass gains with resistance training at 50+. Safe for normal kidneys. Expect 1 to 2 kg of water weight in the first fortnight; it is not fat. Tell the GP before kidney bloods |
| Psyllium | 10 g daily | As above |

Skip: fish oil capsules (no prevention benefit, AF signal at high dose), red yeast rice (an unregulated statin, deaths from a contaminated batch in 2024), high-dose vitamin E, beta-carotene, high-dose niacin, "cholesterol-lowering" herbal products. A cheap multivitamin is harmless but not where the gains are.

## Swap list (same slot, different meal)

**Breakfasts (about 400 kcal, 25 g+ protein)**
- Porridge as above, or made with soya milk for the soya protein
- Overnight oats: 50 g oats, 150 g 0 percent Greek yogurt, 100 ml milk, berries, cinnamon
- 3-egg omelette with spinach, tomato, 20 g feta, 1 slice wholemeal
- 250 g 0 percent Greek yogurt, 30 g oats, berries, 10 g chopped walnuts

**Lunches (about 400 kcal, 25 to 35 g protein)**
- Lentil soup as above (make 4 portions on Sunday)
- Tinned sardines or mackerel on 2 slices wholemeal toast with tomatoes
- Tuna, chickpea, red onion, cucumber salad with 1 tbsp olive oil and lemon
- Leftover dinner protein with a bag of salad and a wholemeal pitta
- Cottage cheese, 2 oatcakes, apple, carrot sticks

**Dinners (about 500 to 550 kcal, 40 to 50 g protein)**
- Chicken, potatoes, two veg as above
- Salmon fillet (120 g), 150 g new potatoes, green beans and peas
- Chilli with 125 g lean mince (5 percent fat) bulked with a tin of kidney beans and a tin of tomatoes, 50 g dry-weight brown rice

**Snacks (100 to 200 kcal)**

## Hunger rules

- Eat the protein and vegetables first at each meal.
- 500 ml of water before the two main meals. Weak evidence, zero cost.
- Sleep. Short sleep adds about 250 kcal a day of intake in trials. If you sleep under 7 hours, fixing that is worth more than any food tweak.
- Plan the evening. If you overeat at night, move calories there deliberately rather than white-knuckling it.
- A bad day is a data point, not a verdict. Log it and carry on. The six-month plateau in most studies is intermittent non-adherence, not metabolism.

## Alcohol

No protective dose. It raises triglycerides and blood pressure, suppresses fat burning, and two pints is 400 kcal you have not got. Suggest none for the first four weeks while habits form, then if you drink, keep it under 14 units a week over at least three days and inside the calorie budget.

## Salt

Cooking from the list above will cut salt from a UK average of about 9 g a day to near the 6 g target without trying. Use lemon, vinegar, garlic, chilli, herbs and spices. A reduced-sodium salt (LoSalt) is fine if your kidneys are normal and you are not on blood pressure tablets of the ACE inhibitor, ARB or spironolactone type; check with the GP if unsure.

## Tracking

Use an app for the daily logging (Nutracheck has the best UK supermarket database; MyFitnessPal is fine if you already have it). Use this repo and Claude for the weekly review. Log everything for the first two weeks, including the slips, because the logging itself is what the evidence rewards. After that, weigh and log protein and calories daily, and stop worrying about the rest.

## Adjustment rules

- Weigh every morning, read the 7-day average only. `python3 tools/progress.py`.
- Losing more than 1 kg a week after the first fortnight: add 100 to 200 kcal.
- Losing under 0.25 kg a week over three weeks: check logging accuracy first (weigh, do not estimate; log oils and drinks), then cut 100 kcal or add a walk.
- Every 5 kg lost, recompute the numbers in `profile.md`; your burn falls as you shrink.
- Retest lipids at 8 to 12 weeks.
