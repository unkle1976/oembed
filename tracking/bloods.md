# Blood tests and vitals

Ask the GP for an NHS Health Check (free for 40 to 74 in England every 5 years) and a fasting lipid panel.
Record results here. Dates in ISO format.

## Lipids (mmol/L)

| Date | Total chol | LDL | HDL | Non-HDL | Triglycerides | Lp(a) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| | | | | | | | baseline, before diet change |

Targets for a man with no diagnosed heart disease (NICE / Heart UK, general population):
total under 5, LDL under 3, non-HDL under 4, triglycerides under 1.7 fasting, HDL over 1.0.
Lp(a) is a one-off test (genetic, does not change with diet). Heart UK recommends everyone has it once.

## Other

| Date | BP (mmHg) | Resting HR | HbA1c (mmol/mol) | Fasting glucose | ALT (liver) | Vitamin D | QRISK3 % | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| | | | | | | | | |

HbA1c: under 42 normal, 42 to 47 prediabetes, 48 or more diabetes.
BP: under 120/80 ideal, 140/90 or more is hypertension (NICE clinic reading).
QRISK3: 10 percent or more over 10 years is where NICE recommends discussing a statin.

## Retest schedule

Lipids respond to diet within 3 to 4 weeks and are stable by about 6 to 8 weeks. Retest at 12 weeks, then at 6 months.
